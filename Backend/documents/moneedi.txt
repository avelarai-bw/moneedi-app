Moneedi Enterprises (Proprietary) Limited is a fully citizen-owned agribusiness specializing in sustainable piggery farming and pork processing through a modern abattoir facility. 
Based in Botswana, the company is founded to address the growing demand for high-quality, locally produced pork while contributing to the GDP, food security, job creation, and rural economic development.
Our dual-operation model includes a biosecurity piggery unit and a compliant abattoir that processes pork for both wholesale and retail markets. 
We pride ourselves on implementing industry best practices in animal husbandry, meat hygiene, environmental sustainability, and ethical business conduct.
Strategic Goals:
Achieve full vertical integration from farm to fork
Expand processing capacity and product range
Enhance food safety and traceability systems
Forge strong partnerships across the agri-value chain
Drive inclusive growth through youth and women participation
Company Background
Moneedi Enterprises (Proprietary) Limited is incorporated in 2022 under the Companies and Intellectual Property Authority (CIPA) of Botswana with company number BW00002290004. 
Our founders, driven by a passion for agribusiness, food production and security, saw a critical gap in Botswana’s pork industry—insufficient local production and heavy reliance on imports.
The company is established with the vision of transforming local pork industry through modern farming methods, value addition, and supply chain development. 
Our operations are located on a fully serviced agricultural plot in South East District, with facilities that include breeding pens, farrowing units, feed storage, and a semi-automated abattoir.
Today, Moneedi Enterprises continues to scale operations while aligning with Botswana’s agricultural diversification agenda. 
Our long-term goal is to become a regional supplier of safe, traceable, and affordable pork products.

Products & Services
Moneedi Enterprises (Proprietary) Limited offers a vertically integrated pork production system encompassing pig farming and abattoir operations. Our core offerings include:
1. Live Pig Production
Breeding and Farrowing – Quality-controlled reproduction using healthy sows and boars.
Weaning and Fattening – Efficient growth and nutrition programs using balanced feed formulations.
Market-Ready Pigs – Supply of live pigs from the farm to the abattoir for processing to the table.
2. Pork Processing (Abattoir)
Slaughtering and primary processing under hygienic conditions.
Carcass cutting and portioning services.
Chilled pork cuts packaged for wholesale and retail markets. 
Bacon, sausages, and smoked products.

3. By-Products & Value Addition
Biogas and energy production
Manure for organic fertilizer use.
Skins and hides production and processing
4. Contract Grower Model (Future Expansion)
Supporting smallholder farmers to raise pigs under Moneedi’s guidance, with guaranteed market access through our abattoir.
